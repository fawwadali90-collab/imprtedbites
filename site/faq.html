We source through licensed import channels, so each product arrives with a sharp readable batch code, a printed expiry date, and an intact factory seal. We only ship stock with several months of shelf life remaining, and the printed expiry date is confirmed per batch before dispatch. Can I return cat food if my cat does not eat it?

Because of food safety rules, opened cat food cannot be resold, so a change of taste alone does not qualify for a return. To be safe, we recommend starting with a small pack when trying a new flavour with your cat. If your order arrives damaged, wrong or past its printed expiry, send us a photo within 48 hours and we will replace it or refund you.

Is stevia a good sugar alternative?

Stevia is a plant derived sweetener that many people use to reduce their sugar intake, and it tastes noticeably sweeter than sugar per spoon. We make no medical or health claims about our products, and sweetness preference is personal, so we suggest trying a small pack first. If you have a medical condition such as diabetes, please consult your doctor before changing your diet.

How should I store cat food and coffee?

Keep dry cat food in its original bag, rolled shut or in an airtight container, in a cool dry place away from sunlight; use it within a month of opening for the best nutrition. Keep coffee sealed tight with a dry spoon, away from the stove and moisture, because humidity clumps it fast. Wet cat food pouches should be refrigerated after opening and used within two days.
