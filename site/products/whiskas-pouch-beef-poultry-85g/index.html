About This Product

The Whiskas Pouch Beef and Poultry 85g is the single serve wet meal that adds what dry food cannot: moisture. Cats are desert animals with a weak thirst drive, and wet food is the easiest way to lift their daily water intake, supporting urinary tract health over the long term. Each 85g pouch serves one meal for an average adult cat, with tender beef and poultry pieces in a savoury sauce that even picky eaters finish. At PKR 240 per pouch, it is an affordable daily topper over dry kibble, and the mixed feeding routine, dry food for grazing plus one wet pouch a day, is what most vets recommend. Serve at room temperature, refrigerate any unfinished portion and use within two days.
