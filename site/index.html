Common Questions

Quick answers before you order.

Is your cat food really imported from Thailand?

Yes. Our cat food range focuses on the Thai manufactured brands Pakistani cat owners trust most, including Me-O and Whiskas. Every pack arrives sealed with readable batch codes and printed expiry dates, and we verify the stock before it lists on the site. If you ever want to confirm a batch, message us on WhatsApp with a photo of the pack and we will check it for you.

How do I know the products are original and not fake?

We source through licensed import channels, so each product arrives with a sharp readable batch code, a printed expiry date, and an intact factory seal. Counterfeit packs usually fail on at least one of these: smudged printing, missing batch codes, or resealed packing. When our full catalog launches, every product page will carry a genuine check guide for that item, and you can always ask us to photograph the actual pack before you order.

Do you offer cash on delivery in Pakistan?

Yes, cash on delivery is available on every order, anywhere in Pakistan. You pay in cash when the courier hands you your parcel, and the total is the PKR price on the site plus the delivery fee stated at checkout. There are no card details, advance payments or hidden charges involved.

How long does delivery take to Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

Orders to Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad are delivered in 2 to 4 working days after dispatch. Smaller cities and towns usually take 3 to 6 working days depending on the courier route. You will get a tracking update on WhatsApp once your parcel leaves our packing station.
